# Bedside

Landing page and product mockup for a paediatric health guidance platform.

Audience: parents of children under sixteen. Business model: one annual fee per household, unlimited assessments, every child covered.

## What is here

`index.html` is a single self-contained page with no build step. Open it in a browser.

It contains, in order:

1. The landing page. Hero line, how it works, and the subscription terms.
2. The parent intake form, filled with a worked example.
3. A sample report with four sections: what you told us, what it likely means, what to do now, and when to worry.
4. The single call to action at the end of the report: book fifteen minutes with a paediatrician.

## Voice rules the copy follows

- Calm, plain, declarative. Short sentences.
- No exclamation marks.
- No medical jargon unless it is explained in the same sentence.
- Never urgent or alarmed. Not commercial in tone.
- The report reflects the parent's own words back to them, and "when to worry" is written as thresholds, not vague symptoms.

## Design notes

- Type: Literata for reading, Source Sans 3 for the form and labels. Loaded from Google Fonts with system fallbacks.
- Colour: a pale grey-green ground, deep slate-green ink, one eucalyptus accent. No red anywhere, so nothing on the page reads as an alarm.
- Light and dark themes are both defined through CSS tokens.
- The example child, parent, and details are made up and the page says so.
