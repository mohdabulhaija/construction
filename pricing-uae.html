What to charge in the UAE, and why.

AED 399per household, per year

Inclusive of VAT. Unlimited written assessments, every child under sixteen at the address. Launch the first cohort at AED 299, locked for life, so renewal never feels like a price rise.

Where AED 399 sits globally

Bedside Benchmark

Blueberry Pediatrics is the closest structural match anywhere in the world: unlimited virtual paediatric care, every child in the household, no per-visit fee. It charges USD 216 a year, about AED 793. That is the ceiling, set by a brand with years of evidence behind it.

The floor is Altibbi at roughly AED 220 a year, but Altibbi is priced for the whole Arabic-speaking region including much lower-income markets. Matching it in the UAE would price Bedside as a commodity.

03

Why 399

  • It is priced against one clinic visit, not against insurance. A parent compares AED 399 for the year to AED 300 for a single paediatric consultation. That comparison wins on its own, with no explanation needed.
  • It clears the commodity band. At AED 220 we look like a cheap teleconsult app. At AED 399 we look like a considered product, while still being half of Blueberry.
  • It breaks even at four questions a year. Four basic-plan co-pays is AED 400. Any parent of a child under five will ask more than four times. That is a sentence a salesperson can say.
  • AED 399 is the natural shelf price. It sits under the 400 threshold, and reads as AED 33 a month, which is small enough in Dubai terms to be a non-decision.
04

The problem with the included call

Unlimited paediatrician calls do not survive contact with UAE clinician rates.

The brief currently promises fifteen minutes with a paediatrician, booked from any report, included in the fee. At UAE teleconsultation rates that single promise can consume the whole subscription.

Annual fee, per householdAED 399
Cost of one 15-minute paediatrician call, at market rateAED 150–250
Assumed calls per household per year2
Clinician cost alone, before any report costAED 300–500

Two calls a year and the product loses money before a single report is written. This is a structural issue, not a pricing one. Raising the price to cover it would push us past Blueberry, which we cannot justify at launch.

The fix, which keeps the promise intact: unlimited written assessments, because those are cheap and they are the actual product. One included paediatrician call per year, then AED 99 for each additional call. That is still well under the AED 149 to 185 the parent would pay anywhere else, so the offer stays generous while the expensive resource is metered.

06

Before this is locked

  • Run AED 299 / 399 / 499 as a landing-page price test before committing. The band is defensible at all three; only real behaviour separates them.
  • Confirm the VAT position. Some licensed healthcare services are zero-rated in the UAE, but a written guidance subscription may not qualify. If 5% applies, AED 399 inclusive nets about AED 380.
  • Confirm DHA and DoH licensing for the paediatrician call. It is the part of the product that is regulated, and it drives the cost line in section 04.
  • Measure the call attach rate from the first cohort. It is the number that decides whether even one included call is affordable.
  • Price the Saudi market separately when you get there. It is the obvious second market and it does not carry UAE cost structure.
